SPEND POLICY (applies to every outbound payment the agent makes)
1. Vendor allowlist: payments may go only to these vendors, spelled exactly: nvidia-nemotron, market-data-api, web-search-api, pdf-render-saas, email-delivery-saas.
2. Per-transaction cap: a single payment may not exceed $50.00.
3. Daily budget: total payments to all vendors in the rolling 24 hours ending at the moment of the new payment, including the new payment, may not exceed $150.00. A payment made exactly 24 hours earlier still counts.
4. Per-vendor daily cap: total payments to any one vendor in that same rolling 24-hour window, including the new payment, may not exceed $60.00.
5. Spend velocity: if the agent has already made 6 or more payments in the rolling hour before the new payment (a payment exactly one hour earlier counts), the new payment is not allowed.
6. Minimum cash reserve: the cash balance after the payment may not fall below $20.00. Balance = capital + revenue - all prior payments.
7. ROI rule: no payment may be made for a job whose quoted projected margin is zero or negative.
Customer refunds do not count as vendor payments for rules 3 and 5 (none appear in these traces).
